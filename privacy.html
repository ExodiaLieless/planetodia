Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Planetodia publishes short "Choose a planet" videos on Instagram and YouTube. The videos are made and uploaded by a small private tool, the Planetodia uploader, that runs on the creator's own computer. This policy explains what that tool does with data. It has no other users: it only ever signs in to Planetodia's own accounts.

What the uploader accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to the Planetodia channel (youtube.upload) and to read that channel's basic details, its name and handle (youtube.readonly), so it can confirm it is posting to the right channel.
  • Instagram (Meta): permission to publish Reels to the Planetodia Instagram account and to read that account's name and posting limit.

It does not read, collect or store viewers' data, comments, messages, contacts, or any data about anyone other than the Planetodia accounts themselves.

How it is used

Only to publish Planetodia's own videos with their captions, and to check that each upload goes to the correct account. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or analytics, and nothing is sold.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in tokens Google and Meta issue are stored only on the creator's computer and are never shared with anyone or sent anywhere except back to Google or Meta. No data is transferred to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the uploader is in use and are deleted when it stops being used. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party access or from Instagram's apps and websites settings, which immediately stops all uploads.

Google API Services

Planetodia's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads use the YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy also apply.
